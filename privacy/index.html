Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: this website has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is your language choice. Personal data reaches us only when you contact us or buy the product — and payments are handled by Whop.

1. Who is responsible

AskIvan, the digital business of Ivan Silva, based in South Florida, United States, is responsible for the data described here. Contact: ia360ivansilva@gmail.com.

2. What this website collects

This is a static website. It has no login, no contact form and no checkout of its own. We do not run analytics, advertising pixels, session recording or tracking cookies of any kind.

3. Your language preference

The site saves one item in your browser: your chosen language (English, Portuguese or Spanish), using localStorage. It stays on your device, it is never sent to us, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data.

4. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record standard technical data — such as IP address, browser type and pages requested — in server logs for security and operation. We do not receive or store those logs.

5. When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive your name, your number or address, and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and to keep the commercial relationship, and we keep it for as long as that relationship lasts.

6. When you buy

Purchases are processed by Whop. Whop collects your account, payment and billing data under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details — never your full card number — so that we can give you access, support you and pay affiliates.

7. If you join the affiliate program

Whop tracks the referrals made through your link and reports the sales attributed to it, so that we can pay your commission.

8. Who processes data for us

  • GitHub Pages — hosting of this site.
  • Whop — payments, receipts and affiliate tracking.
  • WhatsApp / Meta — messages you send us.
  • Our email provider — to read and answer your emails.
  • Third-party tools in the radar — once you click and leave our site, their own policy applies.

9. Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies: consent for the language preference, performance of a contract for delivering what you bought, legitimate interest for security and support, and legal obligation for tax and accounting records.

10. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your personal data, and you can object to processing. Email ia360ivansilva@gmail.com and we will respond within the period the law allows.

If you are in California, you may also ask what we collect and ask us not to sell it. We do not sell personal data. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

The site and the product are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

We and our providers may process data in the United States and in other countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

13. Security

Data travels over HTTPS and access to it is limited to what is necessary. No system is completely secure, so please keep your own passwords and devices protected.

14. How long we keep it

Order and tax records are kept for as long as the law requires. Support conversations are kept while the commercial relationship lasts, or until you ask us to delete them.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Significant changes will be highlighted on the site.
